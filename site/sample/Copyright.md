Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea was published in 1870. This
setting follows the English translation of 1872, and the text is in the
public domain.

The plate facing Chapter I is by Alphonse de Neuville, cut by Henri
Théophile Hildibrand for the illustrated edition of 1871. It is in the
public domain.

The book is set in EB Garamond, which carries the SIL Open Font
License.
