# A note on the text

The words come from the classic-books-markdown collection, which takes
them from Project Gutenberg. That file is one document, and its chapter
headings run at mixed levels and shout their titles in capitals. Here
each chapter is a note of its own, under one heading in the chapter's
own words.

Quotation marks, apostrophes and dashes are set as a printer sets them.
The words themselves stand as the translator left them.
