## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Orphans | 2 lines | `body-orphans` |
| 2 | Widows | 2 lines | `body-widows` |
| 3 | Keep a heading with what follows it | on | `keep-heading-with-text` |

## Orphans and widows

An orphan is a line of a paragraph alone at the foot of a page. A widow is a line of a paragraph alone at the top of a page. `Orphans` and `Widows` set the smallest number of lines that a paragraph leaves on each side of a page break.

When `Keep a heading with what follows it` is on, a heading does not end a page.
