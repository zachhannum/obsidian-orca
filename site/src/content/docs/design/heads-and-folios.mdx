A running head is the line of text at the top of a page, such as the book title or the chapter title. A folio is the page number. The Heads & folios group in the design panel sets both.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Left-page header | `Nothing` | `header-left-page` |
| 2 | Right-page header | `Nothing` | `header-right-page` |
| 3 | Header position | `Outside` | `header-position` |
| 4 | Page number | `Bottom` | `page-number-position` |
| 5 | Number format | `1, 2, 3` | `page-number-format` |
| 6 | Header font | `EB Garamond` | `header-font` |
| 7 | Folio font | `EB Garamond` | `folio-font` |
| 8 | Capitals | `Normal` | `header-caps` |
| 9 | Tracking | `None` | `header-letter-spacing` |
| 10 | Set the running heads in italic | off | `header-italic` |
| 11 | Hide the running head and page number on openings | on | `suppress-head-on-openings` |

## Running heads

`Left-page header` and `Right-page header` each take `Nothing`, `Author`, `Book title` or `Chapter title`. `Header position` puts the running head at the outside edge of the page or in the center.

## Page numbers

`Page number` puts the folio at the top, at the bottom, or at the outside edge. `Number format` sets the folio in arabic numerals or in lowercase roman numerals.

## Fonts

`Header font` sets the font of the running heads. `Folio font` sets the font of the page numbers. Until you pick a font, each one uses the font from the Text group, and its row shows that font. Both pickers list the same fonts as the Text group.

## Capitals, tracking and italic

`Capitals` sets the running heads in small caps or in full capitals. `Tracking` sets the space between their letters. Small caps with a little tracking is the usual setting for a novel. `Set the running heads in italic` sets them in italic.

These three controls also apply to the folio. Capitals do not change digits.

## Openings

An opening is the first page of a chapter. When `Hide the running head and page number on openings` is on, an opening has no running head and no folio.
