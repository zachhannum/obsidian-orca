A running head is the line at the top of a page. A folio is the page number. The Heads & folios group in the design panel sets both.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Left-page header | `Nothing` | `header-left-page` |
| 2 | Right-page header | `Nothing` | `header-right-page` |
| 3 | Header position | `Outside` | `header-position` |
| 4 | Page number | `Bottom` | `page-number-position` |
| 5 | Number format | `1, 2, 3` | `page-number-format` |
| 6 | Header font | none | `header-font` |
| 7 | Folio font | none | `folio-font` |
| 8 | Capitals | `Normal` | `header-caps` |
| 9 | Tracking | `None` | `header-letter-spacing` |
| 10 | Set the running heads in italic | off | `header-italic` |
| 11 | Hide the running head and page number on openings | on | `suppress-head-on-openings` |

## Running heads

`Left-page header` and `Right-page header` each take `Nothing`, `Author`, `Book title` or `Chapter title`. `Header position` puts the running head at the outside edge of the page or in the center.

## The fonts they are set in

`Header font` sets the running heads in a font of their own, and `Folio font` sets the page numbers in one. A head or a folio with no font of its own is set in the book's font.

A running head is usually set in another face than the text, and a folio in another face again. Both pickers offer the fonts the text picker offers.

## The type the heads are set in

`Capitals` sets the heads in small caps or in full capitals, and `Tracking` sets the space between their letters. Small caps with a little tracking is the usual setting for a novel. `Set the running heads in italic` sets them in the italic of the book's font.

The three controls set the folio as well, so the band of type at the head of the page and the number at the foot agree. Capitals and tracking change no digit.

## Page numbers

`Page number` puts the folio at the top, at the bottom, or at the outside edge. `Number format` chooses arabic numerals or lowercase roman numerals for the folio.

An opening is the first page of a chapter. When `Hide the running head and page number on openings` is on, an opening has no running head and no folio.
