A chapter opens on a new page, lower than the text around it. The Chapter openings
group in the design panel sets where the chapter begins, the space above its title,
and the drop cap.

<ChapterOpeningsFigure />

## Where a chapter begins

`Begins on` takes one of three values. `Next page` is the default. `Right page`
leaves a blank page when a chapter falls on a left page. `Same page` runs the
chapter on below the one before it.

## In the book note

Each control that you change writes one key to the frontmatter of the book note.

```yaml
chapter-space-above: 7
chapter-drop-cap: 3
```
