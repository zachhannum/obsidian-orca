By default, a chapter starts on a new page. The Chapter openings group in the design panel sets where the chapter begins, the space above and below its title, and the drop cap.

## Controls

| Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Begins on | `Next page` | `chapter-begins` |
| Space above | 0 lines | `chapter-space-above` |
| Space below | 0 lines | `chapter-space-below` |
| Drop cap | `None` | `chapter-drop-cap` |

## Where a chapter begins

`Begins on` takes one of three values. `Next page` starts the chapter on a new page. `Right-hand page` starts the chapter on a right page. If the next page is a left page, orca leaves that page blank. `Same page` starts the chapter on the same page, below the chapter before it.

## Space and drop cap

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text. A drop cap is a large first letter. `Drop cap` makes the first letter of the chapter 2, 3 or 4 lines tall.

<Page n={9} alt="The first page of Chapter I, A Shifting Reef, with space above the title and a drop cap over three lines." caption="The opening of Chapter I in the sample book." />

## In the book note

Each control that you change writes one key to the frontmatter of the book note.

```yaml
chapter-space-above: 7
chapter-drop-cap: 3
```
