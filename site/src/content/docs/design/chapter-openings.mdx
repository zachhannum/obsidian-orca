By default, a chapter starts on a new page. The Chapter openings group in the design panel sets where a chapter begins, its drop cap and its first line. The space above a chapter title is set in [Headings](/design/headings/).

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Begins on | `Next page` | `chapter-begins` |
| 2 | Drop cap | `None` | `chapter-drop-cap` |
| 3 | Drop cap font | `EB Garamond` | `chapter-drop-cap-font` |
| 4 | Drop cap style | `Normal` | `chapter-drop-cap-style` |
| 5 | First line, capitals | `Normal` | `chapter-first-line-caps` |
| 6 | First line, tracking | `None` | `chapter-first-line-letter-spacing` |

## Where a chapter begins

`Begins on` takes one of three values. `Next page` starts the chapter on a new page. `Right-hand page` starts the chapter on a right page. If the next page is a left page, orca leaves that page blank. `Same page` starts the chapter on the same page, below the chapter before it.

## The drop cap

A drop cap is a large first letter. `Drop cap` makes the first letter of the chapter 2, 3 or 4 lines tall.

`Drop cap font` sets the font of the drop cap. If no font is set, the drop cap takes the body font.

`Drop cap style` sets the weight and the slope of that letter. The control offers Normal, Bold, Italic and Bold italic.

## The first line

The `First line` row sets the capitals and the tracking of the chapter's first line. The line is measured after the text is broken, so a wider page takes more words into capitals.

The engine sets no slope on a first line, so the row offers capitals and tracking alone. The title takes its own capitals and tracking in the Headings group.
