A chapter opens on a new page, lower than the text around it. The Chapter openings group in the design panel sets where the chapter begins, the space above and below its title, and the drop cap.

## Controls

| Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Begins on | `Next page` | `chapter-begins` |
| Space above | 0 lines | `chapter-space-above` |
| Space below | 0 lines | `chapter-space-below` |
| Drop cap | `None` | `chapter-drop-cap` |

## Where a chapter begins

`Begins on` takes one of three values. `Next page` starts the chapter on a new page. `Right-hand page` leaves a blank page when a chapter falls on a left page. `Same page` runs the chapter on below the one before it.

## Space and drop cap

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text. `Drop cap` sets the first letter of the chapter across 2, 3 or 4 lines.

<Page n={9} alt="The first page of Chapter I, A Shifting Reef, with space above the title and a drop cap over three lines." caption="The opening of Chapter I in the sample book." />

## In the book note

Each control that you change writes one key to the frontmatter of the book note.

```yaml
chapter-space-above: 7
chapter-drop-cap: 3
```
