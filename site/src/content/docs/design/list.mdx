A list is a run of items a chapter sets one to a line. You write a bulleted list in markdown with a `-` before each item, and a numbered list with a number. The List group in the design panel sets the marker, the indent and the space between items.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Marker | `Bullet` | `list-marker` |
| 2 | Indent | `1.5em` | `list-indent` |
| 3 | Space between | 0 lines | `list-space-between` |

## Marker

`Marker` takes one of four values:

- `Bullet` sets a filled round mark.
- `Open circle` sets a hollow round mark.
- `Square` sets a filled square mark.
- `None` sets no mark, and the items keep their indent.

`Marker` sets the mark of a bulleted list. A numbered list keeps its numbers.

## Indent and space

`Indent` is the indent of the whole list. The markers hang in it, so the text of every item lines up.

`Space between` is a count of lines of body text. It sits above every item but the first, so a list with 0 lines between items sets one item to a line.
