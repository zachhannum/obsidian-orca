The Headings group in the design panel sets the font, the size, the capitals, the tracking and the alignment of each heading level. A chapter title is a level 1 heading.

## Controls

In a key, `N` is the heading level, from 1 to 6.

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Level | `H1` | none |
| 2 | Font | `EB Garamond` | `heading-N-font` |
|  | Variant | The default variant of the font | `heading-N-font-variant` |
| 3 | Size | `19pt` | `heading-N-size` |
| 4 | Capitals | `Normal` | `heading-N-caps` |
| 5 | Tracking | `None` | `heading-N-letter-spacing` |
| 6 | Alignment | `Left` | `heading-N-align` |

## Pick a level

1. Select a level in the level tabs, from `H1` to `H6`.
2. Change the controls under the level tabs.

If a level has no font of its own, it uses the font from the Text group.

## Capitals and tracking

`Capitals` sets the level in small caps or in full capitals. Small caps are the small capital letters the font carries. Tracking is the space between letters, and titles in capitals are usually set with more of it.

The two controls set every heading at that level, a chapter title and a part title alike. Italic comes from the manuscript: write `# *Chapter One*` in the note.

## Alignment

A heading can be set left, centered or right.
