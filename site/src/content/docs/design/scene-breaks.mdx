A scene break is the space between two scenes in a chapter. The Scene breaks group in the design panel allows you to add ornaments to scene breaks.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mark | `Ornament` | `scene-break-mark` |
| 2 | Glyph | `❧` | `scene-break-ornament` |
| 3 | Font | none | `scene-break-font` |
|  | Style | `Normal` | `scene-break-style` |
| 4 | Size | none | `scene-break-size` |
| 5 | Space above | 1 line | `scene-break-space-above` |
| 6 | Space below | 1 line | `scene-break-space-below` |

## Mark

`Mark` takes one of two values:

- `Space` leaves the space empty.
- `Ornament` decorates the scene break with a symbol.

## Glyph and font

From the `Glyph` row, `❧`, `⁂`, `§` and `✦` can be chosen. A browser can also be selected to choose any glyph from the scene break font.

<Shot
  name="panel-glyphs"
  alt="The glyph browser with the glyphs of Noto Sans Symbols 2."
  caption="The browser, open over an ornamental face."
/>

`Font` controls the font family the glyph is set in. By default, the body font family is used.

`Style` sets the weight and the slope of the glyph. The control offers Normal, Bold, Italic and Bold italic.

`Size` sets the size the glyph prints at. By default, the body font size is used.

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text.
