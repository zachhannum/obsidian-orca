A scene break is the space between two scenes in a chapter. The Scene breaks group in the design panel sets the mark in that space and the space around it.

## Controls

| Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mark | `Ornament` | `scene-break-mark` |
| Glyph | `❧` | `scene-break-ornament` |
| Word | none | `scene-break-word` |
| Space above | 1 line | `scene-break-space-above` |
| Space below | 1 line | `scene-break-space-below` |

## Mark

`Mark` takes one of three values:

- `Space` leaves the space empty.
- `Ornament` sets a glyph in the space. `Glyph` shows when `Mark` is `Ornament`, and offers `❧`, `⁂`, `§` and `✦`.
- `Word` sets a word in the space. `Word` shows when `Mark` is `Word`.

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text.

## In the book note

Each control that you change writes one key to the frontmatter of the book note.

```yaml
scene-break-mark: ornament
scene-break-ornament: ⁂
```
