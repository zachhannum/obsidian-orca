A scene break is the space between two scenes in a chapter. The Scene breaks group in the design panel sets the mark in that space and the space around it.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mark | `Ornament` | `scene-break-mark` |
| 2 | Glyph | `❧` | `scene-break-ornament` |
|  | Word | none | `scene-break-word` |
| 3 | Space above | 1 line | `scene-break-space-above` |
| 4 | Space below | 1 line | `scene-break-space-below` |

## Mark

`Mark` takes one of three values:

- `Space` leaves the space empty.
- `Ornament` sets a glyph in the space. The `Glyph` row shows the glyphs `❧`, `⁂`, `§` and `✦`.
- `Word` sets a word in the space. When you select `Word`, the `Word` row replaces the `Glyph` row. Type the word in that row.

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text.
