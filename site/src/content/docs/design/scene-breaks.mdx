A scene break is the space between two scenes in a chapter. The Scene breaks group in the design panel sets the mark in that space and the space around it.

## Controls

| # | Control | Default | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mark | `Ornament` | `scene-break-mark` |
| 2 | Glyph | `❧` | `scene-break-ornament` |
|  | Font | none | `scene-break-font` |
| 3 | Space above | 1 line | `scene-break-space-above` |
| 4 | Space below | 1 line | `scene-break-space-below` |

## Mark

`Mark` takes one of two values:

- `Space` leaves the space empty.
- `Ornament` sets a glyph in the space.

When the mark is `Space`, the `Glyph` row and the `Font` row are hidden.

## Glyph and font

The `Glyph` row offers the glyphs `❧`, `⁂`, `§` and `✦`, and a field for a glyph of your own. Type a character in that field to set it as the mark.

`Font` sets the font family the glyph is set in. A scene break with no font of its own is set in the body font. Pick a font when the glyph you want lives in another face.

`Space above` and `Space below` are counts of lines of text.
