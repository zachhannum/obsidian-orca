Chapter Twelve

In consequence of an agreement between the sisters, Elizabeth wrote the next morning to their mother, to beg that the carriage might be sent for them in the course of the day.

But Mrs. Bennet, who had calculated on her daughters remaining at Netherfield till the following Tuesday, which would exactly finish Jane's week, could not bring herself to receive them with pleasure before.

Her answer, therefore, was not propitious, at least not to Elizabeth's wishes, for she was impatient to get home. Mrs. Bennet sent them word that they could not possibly have the carriage before Tuesday.

A handle to drag by. Each row the author can move ends in a handle, and a drag starts only there. A touch anywhere else on the row scrolls the list. A heading row has no handle. An entry folds its headings while it is dragged.
A tap turns the book. With a preview of the book open, a tap on a chapter or a heading turns it to that page and closes the drawer. With none, a tap opens the note.
A long press opens the menu. The menu is a sheet from the bottom edge. Open as markdown takes the place of the Mod click. A section's sheet holds what its desktop menu holds.
Nothing waits for a pointer. Open preview and Add to this book are on the book's row at all times, and so are Locate and Remove on a missing note. Collapse all and New book are under the list, where Obsidian puts a drawer's buttons.
