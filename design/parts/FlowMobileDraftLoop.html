One action, both ways. The same place in the header holds Open preview on a note and Open as markdown on the book, so the thumb does not move between the two.
No split on a phone. Open preview to the right and Open manuscript to the left are not offered. A tablet offers both, and the two panes follow each other.
The drawers take no part. The loop opens neither. The navigator and the design panel are there when the author opens them.
