Inspect as on desktop. The target in the header turns it on. A pin opens the pane at the top of the panel's CSS view and turns the panel to that view, and the rules, the crumbs and Add a rule work as they do there.
A tap is the click. A touch screen has no pointer to hover, so no box is outlined before the tap. That is the one difference.
A closed drawer opens for a pin. A pin shows the panel, as a click does on desktop. It is the one thing orca opens a drawer for, because the author asked to see the rules.
A phone has a sheet instead. A tablet has room for the pane beside the page. A phone draws the same pane as a sheet over the foot of the page.
