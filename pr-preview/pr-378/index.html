Each chapter is a Markdown note. One book note stores the book details and the design. Preview the typeset book and export it to PDF, all without leaving Obsidian.

Install in ObsidianRead the docs

Chapters stay notes

The book note lists the chapters in reading order, as links. Its properties are the title, the author, the other metadata and the design.

Write, then format

Write chapters in Markdown. Switch the pane to the preview to see the typeset book, open at the page you are writing.

Design in the panel

The design panel has a control for the trim size, the margins, the type, the headings and more. The preview updates with each change. If your own CSS overrides a setting, its control dims and names the line that overrides it.

Advanced CSS styling

Orca styles the book with CSS. The design panel writes CSS for you, and you can add your own rules to the book note. To find out why something looks the way it does, turn on inspect mode and click it on the page. The panel shows the CSS that styles it, and which rules take effect.

Export for the printer

Export writes the PDF from the pages on screen. Preflight catches missing fonts and images before you export.

On a phone and a tablet

Orca runs in Obsidian on a phone and a tablet. The book and its pages are the same as on the desktop. On a phone the design panel is a drawer and export is a sheet. A tablet can pin the navigator and the panel beside the page.
